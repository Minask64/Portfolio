My journey through study, creativity, and software engineering

I've always had a love for art and worked with computers all the time. Over time, that curiosity became a structured study path in software engineering with a love for design and prototyping. Today, I blend thoughtful design with clean code to make digital experiences that feel alive, and up to date with trends.

Studies

My studies gave me the building blocks of both logic and expression. I learned how systems work, how interfaces communicate, and how to think critically about the tools I use.
