My journey through study, creativity, and software engineering

I started with curiosity and a love for bold visuals. Over time, that curiosity became a structured study path in creative technology and software engineering. Today, I blend thoughtful design with clean code to make digital experiences that feel alive, polished, and a little rebellious.

Studies

My studies gave me the building blocks of both logic and expression. I learned how systems work, how interfaces communicate, and how to think critically about the tools I use. The classroom was where I sharpened my process, but the real learning came when I began applying these concepts to my own creative experiments.

Creative Technologist

As a creative technologist, I sit between art and engineering. I explore motion, interactivity, and unconventional layouts. I use code as a medium to tell stories and to create experiences that surprise and engage, while staying true to a strong visual voice built on black and pink contrast.

Software Engineering

Software engineering gives my work stability and scalability. I write maintainable code, solve problems with clarity, and build systems that can grow. Each project is an opportunity to combine thoughtful architecture with delightful details, ensuring that creative ideas are supported by reliable implementation.

This page is a reflection of my path so far: a blend of study, experimentation, and intentional engineering. I continue moving forward, crafting work that is creative, technical, and confidently framed in the palette I love.
