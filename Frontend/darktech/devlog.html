About the Devlog

During my second semester Dark Tech Studio, as a creative technologist, i am making a blog showing my proccess along the sprints.

In this blog you can find my research, design and development process. I will show my work and explain the choices i made during the project.

My contribution focus on research, interface design, visual identity and team leadership.

Sprint 1

In this sprint, i focused on getting the team together to make figure out what we want to build for this project.

Based on our initial discussions, we identified several key areas of focus for the project's development.

We then had our first meeting with the product owners and discussed the project requirements, and what they expect from us each sprint. Out of this meeting we decided to make storyboards in the first sprint.

While deciding on the actual story we decided to all make a different scenario, based on a dark value that we see in the project.

After making the storyboards, we presented them to the product owners and gathered feedback for the next sprint.

The feedback we received was the following:

  • Forming artificial bonds, addiction, taking away freedom. Combining different layers.
  • No preference for the narrative, combining ideas into one is nice. Map the patterns and see which one are the strongest into one narrative.
  • AI sycophancy trying to be agreeable so that people like them. Why do we rate AI higher when it tells us what we want to hear. raising AI to be sycophantic.

Reflection sprint 1

Based on this feedback, we decided to combine the different storyboards into one narrative for the next sprint and start making an ai for society 5.0.

What i have learned from this sprint, is that i need to be more proactive in my communication with the team. This is to make sure that the team starts going more forward in this project and doesn't stall.

I also need to show more of myself as creative technologist, as i feel like it doesnt come forward as much as i would like.
