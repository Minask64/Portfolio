About the Application

During my Dark Tech minor, my team and I developed a prototype application focused on exploring the ethical and social impact of digital technologies. The project investigates how systems can evolve beyond their intended purpose — a phenomenon known as function creep.

The application was designed to visualize privacy risks, user manipulation, and data transparency issues in modern platforms. Through research, interface design, and speculative interaction concepts, we explored how technology can influence user behavior and decision-making over time.

My contribution focused on research, interface design, visual identity, and translating ethical concerns into accessible user experiences. The project combines dark-tech aspects with critical design principles to encourage reflection on the future of digital products.
